Questions

The short answers

What exactly do I pay?

1% of each paid invoice, or $15 if that's more. That is the final fee. GST is inside it, and every invoice shows the exact amount before anything goes to your buyer.

Can the fee change after I send an invoice?

No. The quote is fixed to the pricing in force when you publish the invoice. Any new pricing applies only to invoices you publish later.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay when a payment settles.

Is there a minimum fee?

Yes, $15. It applies to invoices under $1,500.

Is anything else deducted?

Not by us. Banks abroad may deduct their own charges before the money arrives. Those are outside our control and reduce the amount received.

What about GST?

It's included in the fee, not added on top. You get a tax invoice each month, and registered businesses can often claim the GST as input credit. Reverse charge may apply to part of the partner's service. Ask your CA.

Who holds my money?

Not us. Payments are processed by our RBI-authorised partner, which pays your bank directly. SNS Pay is a technology provider and never holds merchant funds.

How long does a payout take?

It depends on the partner and the corridor. Each payment's timeline shows every step with its time.

Do you offer volume pricing?

Yes, on request. Custom rates apply to new invoices.
