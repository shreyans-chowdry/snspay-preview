How it works

Who does what,in plain English.

Payments are processed by our RBI-authorised partner. SNS Pay is the software around them.

01 / The route

Follow the money

SNS Pay, the software layer

Invoices, compliance checks, deposit matching, the payment timeline and your documents. It sees every step. It never holds the money.

  1. Your buyer

    Pays by local bank transfer from their own bank.

  2. Local bank details

    Issued for you and that buyer by our partner.

  3. RBI-authorised partner

    Receives the funds and converts them to INR.

  4. Your bank

    Paid directly, only to the account in your legal name.

02 / Roles

Two companies, two jobs

Our RBI-authorised partner

Verifies your business, issues the local bank details your buyer pays into, converts the currency, pays your bank and issues the e-FIRA.

SNS Pay

Gives you invoices, payment links, compliance checks, deposit matching, a settlement timeline, documents and integrations. We are software. We are not a bank, we don't hold a payment licence, and your money never sits with us.

Filed

04 / The fee

Taken once, as quoted

When a payment settles, the partner deducts the all-in fee you saw on your quote, then pays you the rest. We never touch your share.

05 / Locked links

Why a link can be locked

Cross-border collections need paperwork: an approved business, an export invoice and an RBI purpose code. A link only opens when all of it is in place, and the buyer sees no bank details until then. Our servers enforce this, not just the interface.

06 / Scope

What it covers

Supported today
Buyers abroad paying Indian businesses for exports of services. Funds land in your Indian bank account in INR.
Not supported
Paying suppliers abroad, personal remittances, and goods shipments that need customs matching.
