01 / The route

Your buyer pays locally.Your bank gets paid directly.

Buyers transfer to local bank details, the way they pay a supplier at home. The partner converts the funds and pays you. We run the software around it.

SNS Pay, the software layer

Invoices, compliance checks, deposit matching, the payment timeline and your documents. It sees every step. It never holds the money.

  1. Your buyer

    Pays by local bank transfer from their own bank.

  2. Local bank details

    Issued for you and that buyer by our partner.

  3. RBI-authorised partner

    Receives the funds and converts them to INR.

  4. Your bank

    Paid directly, only to the account in your legal name.

05 / Safeguards

Money goes wherethe invoice says.

If something doesn't add up, the system stops and asks. It doesn't guess.

  • Deposits are matched, never guessed

    A deposit is applied by its reference, or by one unique exact amount. If it's ambiguous, overpaid or unknown, it's held for a person to review.

  • Payouts go to your account only

    The account must be in your business's legal name, and it's checked at verification. Your payout account can't be changed by an API key or an integration.

  • Every event counts once

    Partner events are signature-checked and recorded once, so a repeated notice can't pay anything twice.

  • Reconciled every night

    Our ledger is compared with what the partner reports. Any difference becomes a visible exception until it's resolved.
