Security

We can't lose moneywe never hold.

SNS Pay never holds merchant funds. Payments are processed by our RBI-authorised partner, which pays your bank directly. Here is how we protect the rest.

Webhooks

Every webhook, in and out, is signed with HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body. Signatures are compared in constant time and rejected outside a 300-second window. Secrets rotate without downtime, and every event is processed once.

Encryption

PAN, IEC and bank account numbers are encrypted with AES-256-GCM at the application layer and masked everywhere. Identifiers are matched by keyed hash. Revealing a masked value requires a recent sign-in, and is logged.

Tenant isolation

Row-level security is on for every table. A test suite tries to read and write across tenants and must fail to do so. Money tables can't be written from the browser at all. Those writes run on our servers through audited database functions.

The compliance gate

Payment links, bank details and API publishes pass the same server-side check. Bank details aren't in the page source until the check passes, and a test asserts exactly that.

Deposit matching

A deposit is applied only by its reference or one unique exact amount. Overpayments, unknown senders and ambiguous amounts are held for review. Nothing is applied on a guess.

Payout account

Your payout account must be in your legal name, and is checked at verification. API keys and integrations can't change it.

Reconciliation

KYB decisions, FX, settlement and e-FIRAs belong to our partner. We keep a ledger that mirrors what the partner reports, and compare the two every night. Differences stay open until resolved.

Retention

Full KYB payloads are encrypted and purged after the retention window. After that we keep only masked values and hashes. Uploaded PDFs are stored privately and always download rather than render on our origin.

SNS Pay

SNS Pay is a technology service provider. Payments are processed by our RBI-authorised partner. SNS Pay is not a bank and never holds merchant funds.
